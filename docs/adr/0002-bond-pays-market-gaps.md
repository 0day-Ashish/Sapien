# Agent Bond pays all Overshoot, including market gaps

The Lead Agent's Bond is slashed first for all Overshoot on its positions, including pure market gaps it did not "cause". The Protocol Reserve pays only for Infrastructure Failure, decided by a fixed rule: no resting stop-loss on HyperCore when the trigger was crossed. This is the core pitch ("the agent's own money is first in line") and makes agents price the risk of thin markets and tight stops. Judging fault case by case would need a dispute process.
